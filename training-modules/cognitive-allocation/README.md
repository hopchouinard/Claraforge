## 🧠 Core Concepts

### The Law of Cognitive Allocation

**Cognitive Allocation** is the strategic distribution of your mental resources across tasks, decisions, and information processing. It's the difference between:

- **Scattered Mode:** Your brain frantically switching between everything
- **Allocated Mode:** Your brain deliberately focusing resources where they'll have maximum impact

### The Three Pillars

1. **Attention Budgeting** - Your attention is a finite resource; spend it wisely
2. **Cognitive Load Management** - Match cognitive intensity to task requirements
3. **Resource Recovery** - Your brain needs downtime to consolidate and restore

### The Hook Examples

- **Calculator Metaphor:** How we allocate mental resources to different mathematical operations
- **GPS Metaphor:** Cognitive load management in navigation and decision-making
- **Fridge Metaphor:** Resource allocation in daily decision-making and habit formation

### The Three Myths

- **Myth 1:** More tools = better outcomes (Reality: Tools are amplifiers, not solutions)
- **Myth 2:** Complexity is inherently bad (Reality: Strategic complexity is powerful)
- **Myth 3:** Cognitive load should be minimized at all costs (Reality: Some load is necessary for growth)
