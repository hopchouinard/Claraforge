2. Create a new notebook titled "Law of Cognitive Allocation - Training Module"

Upload these files in order:

1. `audio-script.md` (primary script)
2. `MODULE-LawOfCognitiveAllocation.md` (core content)
3. `hook-examples.md` (metaphors)
4. `three-myths.md` (myth-busting)
5. `exercise.md` (practical application)
6. `answer-key.md` (insights and reflection)

Upload these files for additional context:
7. `diagrams/hammer-vs-allocation.md` (visual framework)
8. `README.md` (overview and navigation)

### Step 4: Configure Audio Settings

- **Duration:** 25-30 minutes
- **Tone:** Conversational, educational, with Clara's signature sass

- **Pacing:** Moderate pace with natural pauses

### Step 5: Generate Audio

- Use the audio script as the primary guide
- Allow NotebookLM to incorporate content from all sources
- Review and refine the generated audio
- Export in your preferred format
